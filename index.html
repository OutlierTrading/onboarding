05

Your first month

Month one is where free and paid members build the habits that make the community useful. Five things, in this order.

A

Work through the Foundations playlist

Use the Foundations playlist to support the objective you are working on now. Focus on comprehension and application, not volume or checking boxes.

Foundations playlist
B

Begin your trading plan

Do not wait until you feel ready. Start capturing what you already know and let the document expose the gaps. A plan is expected to evolve — the value comes from forcing you to think ahead of time rather than in the moment.

Your plan should eventually capture:

  • Financial and trading goals
  • Time horizon
  • Constraints
  • Experience
  • Strengths and weaknesses
  • Strategies
  • Profit mechanisms
  • Risk limits
  • Portfolio rules
  • Research priorities
  • Execution rules
  • Review process
  • Current learning objectives

Free members can start in any document they already use. Paid members can also begin from the Outlier templates included with their tier.

Trading plan guide
C

Define a six-week objective

Pick a small number of measurable outcomes. Avoid vague objectives such as "become more disciplined" — they cannot be assessed, so they cannot be improved.

  • Complete the first version of your trading plan
  • Review 20 historical trades
  • Build a strategy outline
  • Establish portfolio risk limits
  • Create a repeatable pre-trade checklist
  • Paper trade one researched approach
  • Complete four After-Action Reviews
  • Improve one recurring execution problem
D

Use the community for feedback

Share goals, plans, research, trade reviews, questions, observations, and sticking points in a public room or the paid area included with your tier. The quality of the feedback you get is a direct function of the quality of the question you ask.

Gets you nothing What do you think?
Gets you a real answer My thesis depends on volatility remaining elevated through this event. What evidence would invalidate that assumption?
E

Begin using After-Action Reviews

The AAR is one of the most important processes inside Outlier. Its purpose is to improve the operating system, not criticize the outcome. Everyone can run the process independently; paid members can also post in the dedicated AAR channel. Run these questions against your trades and your process:

What was expected?
What actually happened?
Why was there a difference?
Was the plan followed?
Was the original analysis valid?
Was the structure appropriate?
Was the risk understood?
What should be sustained?
What should be changed?
What should be tested next?

Common questions

The questions new members ask most. If yours is not here, ask in Discord — that is what it is for.

Access and setup

I joined for free. Do I need to connect Patreon to Discord?

No. Join the Discord server and complete Start Here verification to use the public community rooms. Connecting Patreon is required only when Patreon needs to synchronize a paid Discord role.

I joined Discord but cannot see the paid channels. What is wrong?

Almost always the Patreon connection. Connect your Patreon account to Discord, and make sure you are connecting it to the same Discord account you used to join the server. If the two accounts do not match, your paid role will not apply. Also confirm you completed verification in the start-here channel.

Patreon’s step-by-step guide to Discord access ↗

I am not receiving emails from Outlier Trading or Patreon.

Check your Spam and Promotions folders, then add outliertrading@creator.patreon.com, outliertrading@substack.com, and erik@outliertrading.io to your contacts. These addresses cover Patreon membership updates, Outlier Insights newsletters, and direct email from Erik.

Someone messaged me on Discord claiming to be Erik or a moderator.

Ignore it. Impersonation attempts are common — fake accounts copy names and profile images and send direct messages to members. Neither Erik nor the moderator team sends unsolicited Discord DMs. Do not click unfamiliar links, and verify anything questionable in the public or paid channels.

Sessions and schedule

Where do I find this week's livestream links?

Paid members find livestream links in the current schedule post on Patreon. It tells you what is happening that week, what your tier has access to, and which links to use. Free members use the public YouTube channel and the public resources in Section 03.

I cannot find the livestream on the YouTube channel.

Paid livestreams are frequently unlisted, which means they will not appear on the channel at all. Always use the links posted on Patreon to access member sessions.

Which livestreams and workshops does my tier include?

Check Section 03 alongside the current Patreon schedule post. The tier section defines standard benefits; Patreon confirms the current sessions and access links.

When are the Pro Plus workshops?

See the Pro Plus details in Section 03 for the standard schedule and participation expectations. Patreon carries the current topic, required preparation, Zoom link, instructions, and any exceptions.

Where are the recordings?

Paid recordings are in Patreon collections and the archive. Your paid tier determines which recordings are available; the free tier does not include the Pro recording library.

Getting help

Where should I post my question?

Free members should use the public rooms available after verification. Paid members can use the Trading Floor for trade analysis, position structure, management, risk, portfolio questions, and feedback requests; Pro Forums for organized topic-specific discussion; and After-Action Review for reviews and lessons. Ask Erik is available to qualifying paid tiers.

How quickly will I get a response in Ask Erik?

There is no guaranteed response time unless your tier includes priority access.

Do you give trade alerts or tell me what to buy?

No. Outlier is not an alerts community and there are no trade calls to follow. All content is educational, and the purpose is to help you become an independent trader who can research, structure, manage, and review your own decisions. You remain solely responsible for your decisions.

How long until I see results?

There is no guaranteed timeline. Trading and investing involve significant risk, and there is no guarantee of profit, progress, or any specific result — anyone promising otherwise is selling something.

What you control is the process. Members who show their work, ask specific questions, and run After-Action Reviews consistently tend to have a working trading plan and a repeatable review habit within the first six weeks. Set a measurable six-week objective using Section 05 and judge your progress against that, not against your account balance.

Am I expected to keep up with everything?

No. Use what helps you now and skip what does not. Your first week is about access and orientation, not consuming the library — work the checklist in Section 04 and nothing else.

Coaching and Outlier Inner Circle

How do I get started with coaching?

Email erik@outliertrading.io with the subject line "Coaching Intro" and include what made you pursue coaching, your goals, your current experience, your largest trading or process roadblocks, and any relevant materials or background. You will receive a private Google Drive folder, coaching materials and templates, your booking link, and scheduling instructions.

Can I reschedule or carry over a coaching session?

You can book up to 45 days in advance using the same booking link each month. Rescheduling more than 24 hours ahead is generally fine; changes inside 24 hours may forfeit that month's session. Sessions do not carry into the following month.

Membership

How do I cancel my membership?

Paid membership is billed through Patreon, so cancellation is handled in your Patreon account under membership settings—not in Discord. Paid access and the paid Discord role remain active through the end of the current billing period, then the paid role is removed automatically. Free membership has no billing.

If something is not working for you, email erik@outliertrading.io before you cancel. If a different tier is a better fit, that is usually a faster fix than leaving.
